How the layers differ

Baseline airspace comes from OpenAIP. NOTAM information comes primarily from FAA NMS staging. Task relevance means the time, altitude and corridor filters include the item. Activation status is a separate operational interpretation and is never inferred inactive merely because no NOTAM match was found.
